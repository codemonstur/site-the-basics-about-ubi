
---
title: Wages Will Decrease
---

Concern:
Did you know that after women entered the workforce, the money they earned was 
"extra".  After time passed, the market started adjusting to include this income 
in what people could afford.  Now, you need both incomes to support what used 
to be handled by one.  The late stage capitalist market will always adjust for 
that income.  Walmart takes food stamps into account for its employees on how 
much they could pay them.  While UBI could work, it will most likely adjust the 
market to make sure the money was going towards someone up the ladder.

Answer:
This is not what would happen. You are forgetting marginal return.
If you have absolutely nothing, then getting even a little bit of money is 
extremely important to you. That initial purchasing power is the difference 
between eating and not eating, literally the difference between life and death.

With UBI that initial purchasing power is already covered. The added value of 
what your job pays is significantly lower. If your employer attempts to pay 
you too little you can just say no. The salary has to be high enough to justify 
the time and energy investment.
